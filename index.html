Subtitles in the language you choose

Don’t speak the language?
Still watch the video.

Paste a link. Pick the language you want to read. NoSpeaky writes subtitles while you watch — English, Chinese, or whatever you need — then lets you download an .srt file.

Open the watcher See pricing

Example: Chinese show → English subtitles. Or MrBeast on YouTube → Chinese subtitles.

What you do

  1. 1. Paste a video link Or drop a video file from your computer.
  2. 2. Pick subtitle language That’s the language you want on screen — not only English.
  3. 3. Watch Subtitles show on the video. When processing finishes, download an .srt for later.
