How it works

  1. You give it media File upload or a direct video/audio URL you own or have rights to use.
  2. We pull the speech Audio is extracted and run through speech-to-text with timestamps.
  3. You get real captions WebVTT / SRT download plus a player with native CC — not a blurry burned screenshot.
