How it works

  1. 1. Paste a link Public Dailymotion (dai.ly) or a direct .mp4 / .m4a. YouTube often fails from our server — don’t count on it.
  2. 2. Pick a language Spoken language and what you want to read — same list on Watch.
  3. 3. Watch Wait for the timer. Captions stick on the player. Download .srt if you want.
