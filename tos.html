What this product is

NoSpeaky is a personal viewing aid. You paste a public link. We fetch it, write captions of the speech, and can translate those captions into another language for you to read on the player. You can download an .srt for yourself.

We do not sell the source video. We do not claim we own it. We do not re-dub or replace the original audio. We do not run a subtitle file-sharing site.

Your job
