Privacy

Last updated 26 July 2026 · Pi Studios

NoSpeaky is a captioning tool. This page covers what we intend to collect once the transcription engine is live. The public site shell itself is static and does not require an account.

What you send us

  • Video or audio files you upload for captioning
  • Direct media URLs you submit for processing
  • Basic request metadata (time, approximate size, error codes) to keep the service running

What we do with it

  • Extract audio and generate timed captions (e.g. WebVTT / SRT)
  • Store job output briefly so you can download it
  • Delete source media and caption outputs on a short retention window (target: under 24 hours unless you save/export elsewhere)

What we don’t do

  • No sale of your media or transcripts
  • No training public models on your uploads without an explicit separate opt-in later
  • No third-party ad trackers on this site

Rights & responsibility

Only submit media you own or have permission to process. You are responsible for URL targets and copyright. We may refuse or remove jobs that look abusive or unlawful.

Contact

Pi Studios — via pistudios.app. This policy will tighten when accounts, billing, or longer retention ship.
