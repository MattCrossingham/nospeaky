Privacy

Last updated 26 July 2026 · Pi Studios

Plain English.

On-device mode (what’s live now)

  • Your video/audio is processed in your browser
  • It is not uploaded to NoSpeaky servers
  • The speech model may download from a public model CDN (Hugging Face / jsDelivr) and cache on your device
  • We don’t get a copy of your file

What the website itself sees

  • Normal web logs for the static pages (like any website)
  • No subtitle job contents, because jobs don’t hit our API right now

Later features

If we add optional cloud tools (URL helpers, accounts, paid plans), we’ll say so clearly and update this page before anything leaves your device.
