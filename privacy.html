Privacy

Last updated 26 July 2026 · Pi Studios

Plain English version of what happens with your stuff.

What you send us

  • Video links you paste
  • Video/audio files you upload
  • The subtitle language you pick
  • Account email later, when logins exist

What we do with it

  • Read the speech in the video
  • Write subtitles and translate them into the language you chose
  • Show those subtitles on the player and let you download an .srt file
  • Delete the media after a short time (goal: under 24 hours) unless a paid feature says otherwise later

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your videos or subtitle text
  • We don’t put ad trackers on this site today
  • We don’t keep your files forever on purpose

Your responsibility

Only use links and files you’re allowed to use. You’re responsible for the content you submit. We can block abuse (spam, attacks, illegal use).

Money / accounts

When paid plans launch, payment is handled by a payment company (like Stripe). We keep the minimum needed to run your subscription.
